# LyricFluent Growth Audit

**Live → https://eddieatadapty.github.io/lyricfluent-flow-rebuild/**

Five ideas to turn more LyricFluent users into subscribers, each built into a clickable version of LyricFluent's own onboarding. Prepared by Adapty.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Say their goal back to them | +15–20% CR to purchase |
| 02 | Go from the lesson straight to the price | +5–10% CR to purchase · ARPU +10–17% |
| 03 | Make the 66% saving impossible to miss | +10–15% ARPU, from more yearly plans |
| 04 | Keep the proof where they decide, and explain the trial | +5–10% CR to purchase |
| 05 | Catch the people who close the paywall | +10–15% ARPU |

Every price, rating and feature comes from LyricFluent's App Store listing, website or in-app screens. Impact ranges are Adapty’s expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across 30,000+ subscription apps, not measured lift for LyricFluent. They overlap, so they don’t add up.

Static single page, no build step: `python3 -m http.server`.
